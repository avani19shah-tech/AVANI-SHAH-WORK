## One-paragraph orientation

Alexithymia is difficulty **identifying** feelings (DIF), **describing** feelings (DDF), and a thinking style focused on external events rather than inner experience (EOT). Opioid-dependent people show high rates of it, and one influential account (Khantzian's self-medication hypothesis) is that opioids are used partly to manage emotional states the person cannot name or regulate. EFST takes the emotion theory of Emotion-Focused Therapy (Greenberg) and delivers it as a **structured, teachable skills curriculum**: notice the body → name the feeling → build a feelings vocabulary → tell the difference between feelings → find the trigger → understand what the feeling is *for* → express it → regulate it → use something other than the drug. Your study asks whether doing this for 8 sessions measurably lowers TAS-20, and whether HIV status changes how much people benefit.
