| # | [Q → A] | Statement (read aloud) | ✓ Answer | 💡 Say this |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [47 → 48] | "Bipolar I requires a manic episode of at least 1 week." | **TRUE** | …unless hospitalization is required. |
| 2 | [49 → 50] | "A single depressive episode is enough to diagnose RECURRENT MDD." | **FALSE** | Recurrent MDD needs 2 or more episodes. |
| 3 | [51 → 52] | "Benzodiazepines are first-line long-term treatment for GAD." | **FALSE** | SSRIs/SNRIs are first-line; benzos are short-term only. |
| 4 | [53 → 54] | "Anhedonia must be present to diagnose a major depressive episode." | **FALSE** | Either depressed mood OR anhedonia suffices. |
| 5 | [55 → 56] | "Lithium has a wide therapeutic index, so toxicity is rare." | **FALSE** | It's NARROW — needs serum monitoring. |
| 6 | [57 → 58] | "Schizophrenia (DSM-5) requires signs for at least 6 months." | **TRUE** | Includes prodromal/residual; ≥1 month active-phase. |
| 7 | [59 → 60] | "Postpartum psychosis is more common than postpartum depression." | **FALSE** | Psychosis is rare (~1–2/1000); depression far commoner. |
| 8 | [61 → 62] | "ECT is absolutely contraindicated in pregnancy." | **FALSE** | Relatively safe — sometimes even preferred. |
| 9 | [63 → 64] | "OCD is classified as an anxiety disorder in DSM-5." | **FALSE** | DSM-5 gave it its own category (OCRD). |
| 10 | [65 → 66] | "PTSD can be diagnosed only ≥1 month after the trauma." | **TRUE** | Under 1 month = Acute Stress Disorder. |
| 11 | [67 → 68] | "Clozapine is a first-line antipsychotic for schizophrenia." | **FALSE** | Reserved for treatment-resistant (agranulocytosis risk). |
| 12 | [69 → 70] | "Dissociative Identity Disorder is the same as schizophrenia." | **FALSE** | Completely different disorders — classic public myth. |
| 13 | [71 → 72] | "Autism can be reliably diagnosed only after age 5." | **FALSE** | Reliable by ~18–24 months. |
| 14 | [73 → 74] | "The MMSE is a diagnostic test for dementia." | **FALSE** | It's a SCREENING tool, not diagnostic. |
| 15 | [75 → 76] | "SSRIs carry a black-box warning for suicidality in the young." | **TRUE** | Applies to those under age 25. |
| 16 | [77 → 78] | "Delirium and dementia share the same onset pattern." | **FALSE** | Delirium = acute/fluctuating; dementia = gradual. |
| 17 | [79 → 80] | "Negative symptoms respond well to typical antipsychotics." | **FALSE** | They respond POORLY. |
| 18 | [81 → 82] | "Anorexia nervosa has one of the highest mortality rates in psychiatry." | **TRUE** | Among the highest of any psychiatric disorder. |
| 19 | [83 → 84] | "Tardive dyskinesia is an EARLY side effect of antipsychotics." | **FALSE** | It's LATE / delayed-onset. |
